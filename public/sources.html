Dictionary data and licences

Lexi keeps English and Vietnamese definitions in independent lists. Meanings are not necessarily in one-to-one correspondence.

Vietnamese meanings

Lexi adds the Skypedia English–Vietnamese Dictionary (CC BY-SA 4.0) derived from MinhQND Dictionary, including learner-friendly examples, idioms and phrasal verbs. The source describes some examples as generated and reports using GPLv2-licensed OVDP as a reference to correct headwords. See Skypedia's full attribution document and CC BY-SA 4.0. The resulting adapted dictionary content from Skypedia is made available under CC BY-SA 4.0; the application code is separate.

English meanings

Common English entries are enriched with Simple English Wiktionary, extracted via Kaikki/Wiktextract, under Wiktionary's CC BY-SA / GFDL terms. The production build uses the smaller Simple English edition by default to keep Cloudflare build times and mobile downloads practical; full English Wiktionary can be imported separately using the build's source override.

English definitions are derived from Open English WordNet (2025 edition), developed by the Open English WordNet Team under CC BY 4.0. Open English WordNet incorporates material from Princeton WordNet under the WordNet Licence. See full licence and attribution.

Japanese–Vietnamese

Japanese entries, readings, parts of speech and English glosses come from JMdict, the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, used in conformance with the Group's licence (CC BY-SA 4.0), via jmdict-simplified. Hán Việt readings come from KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0), with Vietnamese readings supplied by Minh Chau Pham.

Vietnamese meanings come from the Bunchaway Japanese–Vietnamese Dictionary (CC BY-SA 4.0). Its meanings are keyed by written form only, so Lexi assigns each one to a single entry among homographs and leaves the others with English meanings; the meanings have not been independently verified.

Example sentences and their Vietnamese translations come from Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR), linked to words through Tatoeba's Japanese word index.

About transformations

The Lexi import pipeline normalises spelling, groups definitions under each headword, removes duplicate definitions, keeps per-sense source identifiers and distributes the result as static JSON chunks. Some Skypedia examples are machine-generated and may be inaccurate; they are not independently verified by Lexi. Neither source is endorsed by Lexi. Definitions, IPA and examples may be missing or imperfect.

For the exact versions, view the source manifest.
